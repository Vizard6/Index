❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How to check purity of gold?

Look for BIS Hallmark, 6-digit HUID, and use BIS CARE app.
Difference between 22K and 24K gold?

24K = 99.9% pure (investment). 22K = 91.6% pure + metals (stronger for jewelry).
GST on gold?

3% on gold value + 5% on making charges.
